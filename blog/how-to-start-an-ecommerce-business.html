Starting an online store is easier than ever, and easier to get wrong. This is the sequence we walk new e-commerce clients through.

1. Validate the product first

Sell to your first twenty customers through WhatsApp, Instagram or a simple page before investing in a full store. If they buy again and tell friends, you have something.

2. Sort the paperwork

Business registration, GST and any licences your category needs vary with what and where you sell. Confirm your obligations with a chartered accountant before launch — it is far cheaper than fixing it later.

4. Payments, shipping and returns

  • A payment gateway that supports UPI, cards and net banking.
  • A shipping partner or aggregator with tracking.
  • A written returns and refund policy, visible before checkout.

5. Talk to customers where they are

Order confirmations, delivery updates and "is this in stock?" questions belong on WhatsApp. WhatsApp automation handles them without a team on call all day.

6. Get traffic

Start with search and a complete Google presence, then add Meta ads for discovery and Google Ads for people already searching.

Choosing what to sell

The best first products are ones you understand, can source reliably, can ship without breakage, and can sell at a margin that survives payment fees, shipping, returns and advertising. Write that margin down per product before you build anything.

Your launch checklist

  • Product photos and descriptions written by you, not copied.
  • Prices that include or clearly state taxes and shipping.
  • Payment gateway tested end to end, including a refund.
  • Shipping partner, packaging and a test delivery to yourself.
  • Returns, refund, privacy and terms pages.
  • A WhatsApp number customers can reach, with instant replies.
  • Tracking on the website for orders, so ads can be measured.

The first 90 days

Expect the first months to be about learning: which products sell, which channels bring buyers, which questions customers ask before buying. Answer those questions on the product pages, and the same pages start to rank and convert better.

Questions we get asked

How much does it cost to start?

It varies with the product and model. A custom store with an admin panel starts at ₹45,000 – ₹60,000 with us; see pricing.

Do I need an app?

Not at the start. A fast mobile website is enough; consider an app once customers buy repeatedly — see Android and iOS app development.

Should I start on marketplaces or my own website?

Many start on both: marketplaces for reach, your own site to own the customer relationship and margin.
