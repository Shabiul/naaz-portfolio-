Online store builders have made it possible to start selling in a weekend. A custom e-commerce website takes longer, but fits your business instead of the other way round. Here is an honest comparison.

Where store builders win

  • Fast to launch with a template, a catalogue and payments.
  • Low starting cost — a monthly plan instead of a project fee.
  • A large app marketplace for common add-ons.

Where a custom e-commerce website wins

  • Your own flows — product configurators, booking plus purchase, B2B price lists, quotes.
  • No per-sale platform cut beyond your payment gateway, and no monthly add-on stack.
  • Speed and search — full control of the pages Google reads.
  • Connected systems — orders straight into your CRM, WhatsApp order updates, stock synced with your shop.
  • Ownership — the code and data are yours.

Cost over time

A store builder looks cheaper in month one. Add the monthly plan, paid apps and transaction fees over two or three years and compare that with a one-off build plus hosting. The right answer depends on your order volume and how unusual your process is.

Questions to decide between a store builder and a custom site

  • Do your products need options, configurations or quotes that a standard cart can’t handle?
  • Will you sell B2B with customer-specific prices, as well as retail?
  • Do orders need to flow into your own stock, billing or CRM system?
  • Is search traffic a major source of customers for you?
  • How much will monthly fees and apps cost you over three years?

If most answers are "no", a store builder is probably the right start. If several are "yes", a custom e-commerce website will usually pay for itself.

What every e-commerce website needs, either way

  • Fast product pages with real photos and clear prices.
  • A checkout with UPI, cards and net banking.
  • Visible shipping, returns and contact details.
  • Order updates the customer actually reads — often on WhatsApp.
  • Product pages written for search, not copied from the manufacturer.
