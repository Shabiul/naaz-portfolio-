Meta ads put your business in front of people on Facebook and Instagram who have not searched for you yet. Done well, they are the most efficient way to create new demand. Done badly, they are the fastest way to spend money on likes.

Pick the objective that matches the goal

Meta asks what you want: awareness, traffic, engagement, leads, app promotion or sales. Choose the one closest to the action you will measure — usually leads or sales — because Meta optimises delivery towards it.

Audiences

  • Location, age and interests for a starting audience.
  • Custom audiences from your website visitors and customer lists.
  • Lookalike-style audiences built from your best customers.
  • Meta’s automated targeting, which often works well once tracking is in place.

Creative does most of the work

The image or video, and its first line, decide whether anyone stops scrolling. Test several versions, keep the winners, and refresh before people tire of them.

Click-to-WhatsApp ads

For many Indian businesses the best ad opens a WhatsApp chat instead of a website. Pair it with WhatsApp automation so every chat gets an instant reply and lands in your CRM.

Track properly

Install the Meta Pixel (and, ideally, the Conversions API) on your website so Meta knows which ads led to enquiries and sales, not just clicks.

What it costs with us

Meta ads management is custom-priced by campaign scope; ad spend is paid to Meta separately and stays in your own ad account.

A first Meta ads campaign

  • One objective — leads or sales.
  • One location and a broad audience to start.
  • Three to five creatives: photos, a short video, different first lines.
  • One clear action: WhatsApp, call or a lead form.
  • A daily budget you can run for two weeks without touching it.

After two weeks, keep the creatives that brought enquiries cheaply and replace the rest.

Meta ads mistakes to avoid

  • Boosting posts with no objective or tracking.
  • Narrowing the audience so far that delivery stalls.
  • Judging results after a day or two.
  • Running the same creative for months until people ignore it.
  • Sending clicks to a page that does not match the ad.

From click to customer

The ad is only the first step. Leads from Meta should get an instant reply and land in a CRM, tagged by campaign, so you can see which ads bring customers — not just conversations.

Questions we get asked

How much should I spend on Meta ads?

Start with a test budget you can afford to learn with, then scale only what produces enquiries at a cost that works.

Meta ads or Google Ads?

Meta creates demand; Google captures demand that exists. Most businesses benefit from both — see Google Ads.

Do I need a Facebook page for Instagram ads?

Meta ads are managed through a Meta business account linked to your Facebook page and Instagram profile.
