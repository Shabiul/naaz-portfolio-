Social media management is more than posting. It is choosing where to show up, saying something useful consistently, answering every comment and message, and knowing whether any of it brought in customers.

1. Pick one or two platforms

Instagram suits visual businesses — food, fashion, events, hotels, decor. Facebook still reaches many local and older audiences. LinkedIn suits B2B. Be excellent on one or two rather than absent on five.

2. Plan a simple content calendar

  • Your work — before and after, finished projects, happy customers (with permission).
  • Answers — the questions customers ask you every week.
  • People — the team behind the business.
  • Offers — sparingly, with a clear way to act on them.

3. Treat DMs as enquiries

A message on Instagram is a customer asking to buy. Route DMs and comments to someone accountable — and send the conversation to WhatsApp or your CRM so it is never lost. See WhatsApp automation.

4. Put money behind what already works

When a post performs well organically, that is your best ad. Meta ads turn proven posts into predictable reach.

5. Measure enquiries, not likes

Track messages, calls and website visits that came from social — the numbers that turn into revenue.

A weekly social media routine

  • Monday — plan the week’s posts from your calendar.
  • Daily — reply to comments and messages within hours.
  • Twice a week — post: one piece of work, one answer or tip.
  • Friday — note which posts drove messages or visits.

Content ideas that work for local businesses

  • Behind the scenes of a real job.
  • A customer question, answered in 30 seconds.
  • Before and after.
  • A new offer, with a WhatsApp button to claim it.
  • Your team, by name.

Social media and search

Social profiles are part of your digital presence: the same name, logo and details as your website and Google profile help customers and AI assistants recognise you as one business.

Questions we get asked

How often should a small business post?

Consistency beats frequency: a pace you can keep every week.

Is organic social still worth it?

Yes, for trust and for finding which content to put ad money behind.

Should I use reels or posts?

Short video usually reaches more people; posts and carousels suit details and prices. Use both.
