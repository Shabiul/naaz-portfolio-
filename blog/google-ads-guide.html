Google Ads puts you at the top of Google when someone is actively searching for what you sell. Because the intent is already there, it is often the fastest way to turn budget into enquiries — if the campaign is set up carefully.

Start with Search campaigns

Search ads show as text results for the keywords you choose. They are the clearest place to start: you pay when someone clicks, and you can see exactly which searches led to enquiries.

Keywords and match types

  • Choose keywords that signal buying intent — "AC repair near me", not "how ACs work".
  • Use match types to control how closely a search must match your keyword.
  • Add negative keywords to stop paying for searches like "free", "jobs" or "DIY".

Target where you actually serve

Set locations to the areas you can serve, and schedule ads for the hours you can answer the phone.

The landing page matters as much as the ad

Send each ad to the page that matches it — the specific service, not the home page — with an obvious way to call, WhatsApp or enquire. A slow or vague page wastes every click. See web development.

Track conversions from day one

Set up conversion tracking for calls, form submissions and WhatsApp clicks. Without it, you are judging the campaign on clicks, which tells you nothing about customers.

Google Ads campaign types, briefly

  • Search — text ads on search results. The best start for most businesses.
  • Performance Max — Google spreads one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Maps and more.
  • Display — image ads across websites, mainly for reminding past visitors.
  • Video — ads on YouTube.

Writing ads that get the right clicks

  • Put the service and the place in the headline — "AC repair in JP Nagar".
  • Say what makes you the safe choice — response time, guarantee, reviews.
  • Show prices where you can, to filter out the wrong clicks.
  • Use call, location and sitelink assets so ads take more space.

Weekly Google Ads checks

  • Search terms report — add negatives for irrelevant searches.
  • Cost per enquiry by campaign and keyword.
  • Pause what spends without enquiries; move budget to what works.
  • Landing page speed and form submissions.

Questions we get asked

How quickly does Google Ads work?

Ads can show within a day of approval; it takes a few weeks of data to optimise well.

Is SEO better than Google Ads?

They do different jobs: ads buy visibility now, SEO earns it over time. Most businesses use both.

What budget does Google Ads need?

Enough clicks per week to learn from in your market; we set a capped test budget first and scale on results.
