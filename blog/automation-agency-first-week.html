Most businesses that call an automation agency already know something is leaking: enquiries answered the next morning, leads typed into a notebook, customers who never got the follow-up. The mistake is trying to automate everything at once. These are the workflows we set up first as an automation company — in roughly this order, because each one pays for the next.

The five workflows worth automating first

1. Instant replies to every enquiry

A customer who messages at 9pm and hears back at 11am has usually asked your competitor too. A WhatsApp chatbot that answers immediately — prices, availability, location, timings — from your own information closes that gap without anyone being awake.

2. Lead capture straight into a CRM

Every conversation should land as a record: name, number, what they asked, where they came from. No copying from WhatsApp into a spreadsheet. If you do not have a CRM yet, this is the moment to decide what kind you need.

3. Booking and appointment requests

Let the customer pick a slot or request a booking in the chat, and send it to your team already structured. Hotels and resorts we work with — Coorg Jungle Mist and The Grange Hotel in Ooty among them — use WhatsApp automation for exactly this: guest enquiries and booking requests handled on WhatsApp.

4. Reminders and follow-ups

Appointment reminders, "are you still interested?" follow-ups, and a nudge when a quote has gone quiet. These are the messages people intend to send and forget to.

5. Website enquiries to WhatsApp

A form on your website that nobody checks is worse than no form. Routing every website enquiry straight to WhatsApp — where your team already lives — is one of the cheapest wins there is.

Automation agency or do it yourself?

Do-it-yourself automation tools are genuinely good for simple, single-step flows between apps you already use. An automation agency earns its fee when:

  • the flow runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform and needs approved templates and a verified number;
  • replies need to understand the customer — which is where an AI agent comes in — not just match a keyword;
  • it has to connect to a CRM, a booking system or an admin console that is specific to your business;
  • you want someone accountable when it breaks at 2am.

Questions we get asked

Will automation make us sound robotic?

Only if it is built badly. Good automation answers the routine questions instantly and hands anything unusual to a person, with the conversation so far attached.

Can we keep our current WhatsApp number?

Yes. We set the system up on your own number, so customers keep messaging the number they already have.
