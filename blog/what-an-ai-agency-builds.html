"AI agency" has become one of those phrases that can mean anything — from someone reselling a chatbot subscription to a team that designs, trains and runs AI systems against your actual business. If you are comparing AI companies, it helps to know what the work really is. This is what we build at Naaz AI Labs, an AI and automation company in Bengaluru, and how we would vet an AI agency if we were the client.

What an AI agency should actually deliver

The useful test is simple: after the project, does something answer, decide or act on your behalf that a person used to? For most businesses we work with, that comes down to four kinds of system.

1. AI agents on WhatsApp and web chat

An AI agent that replies to every enquiry, day or night, in the customer's language, answering from your own documents — price lists, policies, brochures — rather than from the open internet. It qualifies the lead, books the appointment and hands your team a warm conversation. This is what Lynq, our AI automation platform, does on WhatsApp, a website chat widget and phone lines.

2. AI voice calling agents

The same intelligence on a phone line: answering inbound calls, making outbound follow-up calls, and scoring each lead hot, warm or cold so your sales team calls the right people first.

3. Multi-agent AI infrastructure

Several AI agents coordinated behind one workflow — one qualifies, one schedules, one drafts the quote, one updates the CRM. We build these with agent frameworks such as CrewAI on top of models from Google, OpenAI and Anthropic, chosen per job rather than by habit.

4. The systems around the AI

An AI agent is only as good as what it is connected to. In practice an AI agency also builds the CRM the leads land in, the website the enquiries come from, and the integrations between them.

How to tell a real AI company from a reseller

Ask these before you sign anything:

  • Can you show me live systems? Not a demo video — working sites and agents for real clients. Ours are on the proof of work page, each linked to the live site.
  • Where does the AI get its answers? It should answer from your documents and say so when it doesn't know, rather than inventing a price.
  • Which languages does it handle? In India this matters. Our agents work in six Indian languages, detected automatically mid-conversation.
  • Who owns the number, the data and the conversations? You should. Our WhatsApp deployments run on your own number, on the official WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • What happens if it never goes live? Our proposals carry a written 100% refund if the system does not go live.

Questions we get asked

Is an AI agency the same as an automation agency?

They overlap. Automation runs fixed rules — when a form arrives, send this message. AI handles the parts that need judgement — reading what the customer actually asked and answering it. Most good systems use both.

Do I need a new website first?

No. An AI agent can go live on your existing WhatsApp number and website. If the website is the bottleneck, we fix that as a separate piece of work.

How long does it take?

A Lynq subscription is live in seven days. Custom AI and multi-agent builds are scoped first, and the timeline and payment milestones are agreed before any development begins.
