People no longer search only on Google. They ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity, and they read the AI summary at the top of the results page. So "ranking" now means two things: appearing in the list of links, and being the source an AI answer quotes. Here is how the four names for this work fit together, and what we actually do.

What we do — on our own site first

Apart from the last, you can check every item below on naazailabs.com itself; we would not sell a technique we don't use.

  • Crawlable HTML. Every page is built to complete static HTML at build time, so search engines and AI crawlers that never run JavaScript still read everything.
  • Structured data. Organization, Person, FAQPage, BlogPosting and breadcrumb markup, all pointing at one identity, so engines know who wrote what.
  • An llms.txt file — a plain-text summary of the company, services and prices for AI systems to read.
  • AI crawlers welcomed by name in robots.txt, not blocked by accident.
  • Question-led content. FAQ pages and posts that answer the exact questions customers ask, in the words they use.
  • Specific, citable facts. Real prices, real timelines and real projects — the kind of detail an AI answer can quote.
  • Local presence. A complete, accurate Google Business Profile — often the fastest win for a local business.

What SEO rankers should never promise

If an agency guarantees the number-one position on Google, walk away. Nobody controls the ranking, and the shortcuts that promise it — bought links, keyword stuffing, copied content — get sites penalised. What a good SEO team can promise is the work: fixing what holds you back, publishing what earns rankings, and reporting honestly on the results.

Questions we get asked

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes and a Google Business Profile can show results within weeks; content usually takes a few months to build rankings. Anyone quoting a guaranteed date is guessing.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. AI assistants lean heavily on pages that already rank and are clearly structured, so good SEO is the foundation GEO is built on.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Not always. If the current site is slow or hard to crawl, rebuilding can be the cheaper route — we cover that in website builders vs a custom website.
