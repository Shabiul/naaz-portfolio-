Once a business hires more than a handful of people a year, CVs in email inboxes and shortlists in spreadsheets start losing good candidates. An applicant tracking system — ATS — puts every opening, applicant and interview in one place.

What an ATS does

  • Publishes openings to your careers page and job boards.
  • Captures every application with the CV attached.
  • Moves candidates through stages — screened, interviewed, offered, joined.
  • Schedules interviews and reminds interviewers.
  • Keeps every note and decision with the candidate.

Features that matter in India

  • WhatsApp updates — candidates often reply faster on WhatsApp than to email.
  • High-volume screening — knock-out questions and filters for roles with hundreds of applicants.
  • Multiple locations — openings and hiring managers by branch.
  • Data protection — candidate data handled in line with the DPDP Act.

Buy or build?

A ready-made ATS suits standard corporate hiring. A custom ATS makes sense when hiring is core to the business — staffing, security and facilities, healthcare, field teams — or when it must connect to your own HR, payroll or client systems.

How an ATS improves hiring

  • Nothing gets lost — every applicant is in one list, with their CV and history.
  • Faster responses — candidates hear back quickly, so good ones don’t accept elsewhere first.
  • Fairer decisions — every candidate goes through the same stages and questions.
  • Visible pipeline — managers see how many candidates are at each stage, per opening.

ATS features checklist

  • A careers page and application form you control.
  • Custom pipeline stages per role.
  • Interview scheduling and reminders.
  • Notes, ratings and feedback from each interviewer.
  • Email and WhatsApp messages to candidates from templates.
  • Offer letters and onboarding hand-off.
  • Reports on time to hire and where good candidates came from.

Connecting the ATS to the rest of the business

A custom ATS can hand a hired candidate straight into your HR or payroll tool, or — for staffing and facilities firms — into the client deployment roster. That is where a custom build earns its cost, through integrations with the systems you already use.

Questions we get asked

Can an ATS screen CVs with AI?

It can rank and summarise CVs against your criteria. Final decisions should stay with a person.

Can it post to job boards automatically?

Where a job board offers an integration, yes; otherwise the ATS generates the listing for you to post.
