Your digital presence is everything a customer finds when they look you up — the website, the Google listing, reviews, social profiles, and now what an AI assistant says about you. Here is a 90-day plan that builds it in the right order.

Days 1–30: the foundations

  • A fast, mobile-friendly website with clear services, prices where you can, and one obvious way to enquire.
  • A verified, complete Google Business Profile.
  • The same business name, address and phone number everywhere it appears.

Days 31–60: trust

  • Ask every happy customer for a review, and reply to every review — good or bad.
  • Real photos of your work, your team and your premises.
  • A proof of work or case-study page with real, named projects.

Days 61–90: reach

  • One useful article or page per week answering a question customers actually ask.
  • Consistent posting on the one or two social platforms your customers use — see social media management.
  • Structured data and clear facts so AI assistants can cite you — see SEO, GEO and AEO.

Your website is the hub

Social profiles, listings and ads all point somewhere. Make that somewhere your own website — fast, clear, with a page for each service and each area you serve — not a profile on a platform you do not control.

Be visible in AI answers too

More people now ask an AI assistant "who is a good AI agency in Bengaluru?" or "best resort near Madikeri". Assistants favour businesses with clear, consistent facts across the web: the same name, services, location and prices on your website, your Google profile and your listings. Specific, quotable detail helps — see SEO, GEO and AEO.

Signs your digital presence is working

  • More people search for your business by name.
  • More calls, direction requests and website clicks from your Google profile.
  • More enquiries that mention finding you online.
  • Reviews arriving steadily, not in bursts.

Questions we get asked

Do I need to be on every social platform?

No. Be excellent on the one or two your customers use.

How do I know it is working?

Watch enquiries, calls from Google, and searches for your business name over the 90 days.

Should I make content in Indian languages?

If your customers search in them, yes — a page in the language your customers use is easier to find and trust.
