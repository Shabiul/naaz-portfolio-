A WhatsApp automation flow, end to end

  1. A customer messages — from your website button, a click-to-WhatsApp ad, or your Google profile.
  2. An instant reply answers their question from your information.
  3. The lead is captured — name, number, what they want — into your CRM.
  4. A booking or quote is offered in the chat.
  5. A person takes over when the customer is ready or the question is unusual.
  6. Follow-ups go out automatically if the customer goes quiet.

WhatsApp automation best practices

  • Always offer a way to reach a person.
  • Only message people who have opted in, and make opting out easy.
  • Keep broadcast messages useful and infrequent.
  • Use the customer’s language where you can.
  • Review real conversations weekly and improve the answers.
