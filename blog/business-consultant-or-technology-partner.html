Many owners call a business consultant when the real problem is a system: enquiries going unanswered, leads lost in a notebook, staff re-typing data between tools. A consultant can tell you that. A technology partner can tell you and then fix it. Knowing which you need saves months.

When a business consultant is the right call

  • You are unsure what to sell, to whom, or at what price.
  • You need a business plan, funding pitch or market entry strategy.
  • The problem is organisational — roles, hiring, incentives.

When you need a technology partner

  • You already know where it hurts: slow replies, lost leads, manual follow-ups.
  • The fix is a website, a CRM, an automation or an AI agent.
  • You want one team accountable from the advice to the working system.

What AI and automation consulting looks like

Our consulting starts with how your business actually works — who enquires, how they are answered, where the hand-offs break — and ends with a scoped proposal: what to build, what it costs, and what it will change. The six steps are agreed up front: discover, strategize, design, develop, test and launch, then support and improve.

If the answer is "you do not need software", we say so. If it is, the same team builds it — automation, a CRM or an AI employee.

The questions a good business consultant asks

  • Where do your customers come from today, and what does each one cost to win?
  • How long does a new enquiry wait for a first reply?
  • What happens to an enquiry that does not buy straight away?
  • Which tasks does your team repeat every day?
  • Which numbers do you look at every week — and which do you wish you could see?

If the answers point to slow replies, lost leads and manual work, the fix is a system, and a technology partner can build it directly.

Advice that ends in a working system

The gap between "you should automate your follow-ups" and follow-ups that actually happen is where most consulting stalls. We close it by scoping the fix with prices and timelines, then building it — business automation, a CRM, or an AI employee on WhatsApp.

How to judge the result

Agree the measure before work begins — reply time, enquiries captured, follow-ups sent, hours saved — and check it after the first month. Advice you cannot measure is hard to act on.
