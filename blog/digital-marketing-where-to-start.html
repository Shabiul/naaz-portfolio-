Digital marketing is a long list of channels, and most small businesses try them in the wrong order — paying for ads before the website can turn a click into an enquiry. This is the order we recommend, cheapest and most durable first.

1. Get found locally

A complete, verified Google Business Profile is often the single cheapest win for a local business. It puts you on Google Maps and in local results.

2. Make the website convert

Every channel below sends people to your website or WhatsApp. If the site is slow, unclear or has no obvious way to enquire, every rupee after this is wasted. See what web development costs.

3. Answer every enquiry, fast

Marketing creates enquiries; WhatsApp automation makes sure none of them waits until morning.

4. Earn search traffic

SEO and useful content take months to build, but keep paying back. The same work now helps you appear in AI answers — see SEO, GEO and AEO.

5. Then buy attention

With the basics in place, paid ads scale what already works: Google Ads for people searching for you, Meta ads for people who don’t know you yet.

A simple digital marketing budget split

Treat the first three months as building and learning, not scaling:

  • Fixed set-up — Google Business Profile, a website that converts, tracking.
  • Ongoing work — SEO and content, posting on one or two social platforms.
  • Test budget — a small, capped ad spend to find which channel produces enquiries at a cost you can afford.

Only once a channel shows a reliable cost per enquiry does it deserve a bigger budget.

Common digital marketing mistakes

  • Running ads to a home page instead of the page for that service.
  • Measuring likes, impressions or clicks instead of enquiries.
  • Changing everything every week, so nothing has time to show results.
  • Spending on five channels at once, each too thinly to learn anything.
  • Leaving enquiries unanswered for hours — the most expensive mistake of all.

Make marketing and operations one system

Every enquiry from every channel should land in one place — a CRM — tagged with where it came from. That is how you learn which marketing works, and it is what we build alongside Google Ads and Meta ads management.
