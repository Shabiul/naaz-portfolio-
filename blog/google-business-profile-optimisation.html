Google Business Profile — still widely called GMB, Google My Business — decides whether you appear when someone nearby searches for what you sell, and on Google Maps. For a local business it is often the highest-return hour of marketing there is.

The optimisation checklist

  1. Verify the profile. An unverified profile can’t be fully managed.
  2. Choose the right primary category, then add secondary ones that genuinely apply.
  3. Make name, address and phone match your website and every other listing exactly.
  4. Set accurate hours, including holiday hours.
  5. Write a plain description of what you do and where — no keyword stuffing.
  6. List your services or products, with prices where you can.
  7. Add real photos of your premises, team and work, and keep adding them.
  8. Ask for reviews from every happy customer, and reply to every review.
  9. Post updates — offers, events, new work.
  10. Link to the right page — the website, plus a booking or appointment link if you take them.

What not to do

  • Add keywords to your business name — it breaks Google’s guidelines and risks suspension.
  • Buy or swap reviews.
  • Use a virtual office address you don’t actually serve customers from.

How Google ranks local results

Google describes local ranking in three parts: relevance (how well your profile matches the search), distance (how far you are from the searcher) and prominence (how well known you are — reviews, links, activity). You can’t change distance, but a complete, active profile improves the other two.

Getting more reviews, the right way

  • Ask in person at the moment of delight, then send the review link on WhatsApp.
  • Make it one tap — a direct link to your review form.
  • Reply to every review, thanking good ones and resolving bad ones in public.
  • Never offer rewards for reviews or post them yourself.

Keep it active

Add new photos, post an update and answer new reviews every week or two. An active profile signals a living business — to Google and to the customer comparing you with the competitor down the road.

Questions we get asked

How long until my profile ranks?

A complete, verified profile can start appearing within weeks; reviews and activity build rank over time.

Do reviews affect ranking?

Yes — review count, rating and your replies are part of how Google judges local prominence.
